# Shot list

Every photo slot on the site, from [data/photos.ts](../data/photos.ts). Until a real photo arrives, each slot shows a designed placeholder with no faces.

**Rules for every photo**
- Real photographs only: no AI images of Joyce or anyone else.
- The campaign must hold the rights. Record who took each photo and the licence.
- Use natural light, people working rather than posing, and Joyce in the frame.
- Send originals at the minimum size or larger. JPEG or HEIC is fine. Don't apply filters.
- Drop files into `public/images/<category>/` and tell us which slot each one fills.
