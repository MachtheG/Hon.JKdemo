## 4. Conflicts and errors found (not used on the site)

1. **Mzalendo's 12th-Parliament page credits her with 242,775 votes, UDA and Gazette Notice 9951.** Those are the 2022 winner's figures. TransTally's 69.57% turnout uses the 2022 register. Neither is used.
2. **Education:** the 2017 and 2022 Parliament profiles disagree. The school is listed as "Mugango" vs "Mucamco", and the JKUAT diploma has different titles and years. The 2022 profile adds a Moi University degree (2018–2022).
3. **Council role:** she is listed as elected councillor and Deputy Mayor from 2007 in one source and as a nominated councillor in another. Her term as mayor appears as 2011–2013 in some sources and 2007–2013 in others.
4. **Date of birth:** Wikipedia gives 1 July 1981 but cites nothing for it, so it won't be published.
5. **2022 nomination contest:** news reports describe a disputed UDA primary for Bomet Central. Per the brief there's no restriction, but the site will mention only the 2022 Senate nomination, which keeps the site to positive, sourced claims.
